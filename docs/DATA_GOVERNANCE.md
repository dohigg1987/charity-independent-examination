# Data governance

## Ownership and purpose

The practice operating Clarity IE is responsible for defining controller and processor roles, the lawful basis for each processing purpose and required privacy information. The system is intended to manage UK charity independent-examination engagements, client evidence, communications, workpapers and quality records.

Before live use, complete and approve a data-protection impact assessment covering identity, client uploads, special-category or criminal-offence data that may occur in evidence, hosting locations, subprocessors, access monitoring, retention and breach response.

## Classification and minimisation

- Restricted: client evidence, workpapers, trial balances, draft accounts, messages and personal data.
- Confidential: user registers, engagement metadata, review notes and audit records.
- Internal: methodology and operating documentation.

Collect only information needed for the engagement. Do not use real client files for training, demonstrations or testing. Avoid free-text personal data where a structured field is sufficient.

## Retention and disposal

The practice must approve a retention schedule that reflects professional, contractual, legal and insurance requirements. A six-year engagement-file baseline may be configured where the approved policy supports it, with documented holds for complaints, claims, investigations or regulation. Permanent-file records should be reviewed annually and superseded records retained only as required by policy.

At the end of retention, delete database records, objects and recoverable copies through the hosting provider's approved process and record the disposal. Product-level automated retention and legal-hold orchestration remain a roadmap control; until implemented, the data owner must operate and evidence a scheduled manual review.

## Data-subject and client requests

Verify the requester and scope before exporting, correcting, restricting or deleting information. Search active and archived engagements, permanent files, messages, uploads, user registers and audit evidence. Preserve records subject to a legal or professional hold. Record the request, decision, approvals, export method and completion date.

## Quality evidence

The audit trail and sign-offs are evidence of actions in this application. They do not replace supervisory judgement, engagement documentation, secure backups or independent assurance over the hosting platform.
